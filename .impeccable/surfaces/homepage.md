## Scope

Homepage (single-page marketing site), Persuade mode. Redesign of Tech Care Zone's repair-shop landing page, replacing the current generic tech-store look while preserving all real content from content.md (services, devices, reviews, contact, hours).

Audience: Powai/Hiranandani-area residents with a broken Apple device or laptop, comparing local repair shops, deciding whether to call/WhatsApp/walk in. Job: get convinced this shop is expert and trustworthy, then act (call, WhatsApp, or book). Proof/content available: 6 named reviews, 5000+ devices repaired, 4.9★, 6-month warranty, free diagnosis, transparent pricing, real address/hours. Constraint: no fabricated stats beyond what's given; keep review text verbatim.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The site itself performs a teardown on the visitor's problem — every section reads as a step in an illustrated service manual (numbered callouts, torque/spec notation, dashed disassembly arrows) instead of the category-default glossy "Apple Store clone" tech-repair site.

**OWN-WORLD:** Schematic-paper ground (warm off-white, faint graph-ruling), graphite ink for text/linework, safety-orange as the single primary-action/callout accent (booking CTAs, numbered tags), steel-blue as the secondary accent for the laptop-brand family (vs. orange for Apple family), confirm-green reserved for warranty/trust badges only. Display type: Space Grotesk (geometric, drafting-instrument letterforms echoing technical stencil lettering on real teardown/patent diagrams). Body: DM Sans (plain workhorse so diagram elements carry the personality). Callout/spec numerals: IBM Plex Mono, justified by its tabular-figure design brief — used only where real numbered specs/tags appear (screw-style step numbers, price figures), not as generic UI chrome.

**STORY:** Visitor lands, immediately sees their exact problem named as a diagnosed "fault" with a numbered callout pointing to the fix and its cost-transparency promise; scrolls through a manual-style breakdown of services (Apple sheet, Laptop sheet) each drawn like an exploded parts diagram; hits a "warranty certificate" style proof block (6-month warranty, 4.9★, 5000+ repaired) stamped like a manual's inspection seal; reads real reviews formatted as service log entries; reaches a "repair order form" styled like a manual's intake/claim ticket; closes on a fixed manual-tab quick-contact strip.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed schematic-paper hero. Left: headline set as a diagram title block ("FIG. 1 — DEVICE REPAIR") with dashed leader lines pointing to floating device silhouettes (phone/laptop) each carrying numbered fault callouts (1 Screen, 2 Battery, 3 Water damage...). Right/below: a torque-spec-style stat strip (5000+ / 4.9★ / Same-day / 6-month) set as a spec table. Primary CTA ("Book a Repair") styled as a stamped work-order button in safety orange, top-right nav plus hero.

**FORM:** Exploded Teardown Manual — my top-ranked grounded candidate (index 1 of 7), chosen over the assigned roll (Diagnostic Instrument Panel, index 6). Seed key bb8de9c8.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
