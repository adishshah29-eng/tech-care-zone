## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (client decision; deploy target: Netlify, matching the current live reference)

## Users

Residents and professionals in and around Powai, Mumbai (Hiranandani Gardens, Chandivali, Kanjurmarg, and nearby areas) who own Apple devices (iPhone, iPad, MacBook, iMac) or laptops from Lenovo, Dell, HP, Acer, or Asus and need repair — screen damage, battery issues, water damage, hardware failure, or performance upgrades. They are deciding between local repair shops and evaluating trust, speed, and price before walking in or calling.

## Product Purpose

A local repair-shop marketing/booking site for Tech Care Zone (TCZ). It exists to build enough trust that a visitor picks up the phone, messages on WhatsApp, or fills the booking form instead of going to a competing repair shop. Success = repair bookings (calls, WhatsApp chats, form submissions) and walk-ins from people who found the shop online.

## Positioning

Powai's Apple-specialist repair centre that also services all major laptop brands (Lenovo, Dell, HP, Acer, Asus) — a combination most single-brand repair shops in the area do not offer. Backed by genuine-quality parts, a 6-month warranty on every repair, free diagnosis, transparent pricing shown before work starts, and same-day (often 1–2 hour) turnaround.

## Product Principles

1. Trust first — real reviews, real address, warranty and transparent pricing must read as credible, not generic.
2. Local relevance — Powai/Hiranandani/Chandivali/Kanjurmarg references matter to the target audience; this is a neighborhood shop, not a national chain.
3. Speed to action — every section should make it easy to call, WhatsApp, or book, since the business converts on quick contact, not long consideration.
4. Dual expertise, clearly separated — Apple specialization and all-laptop-brand coverage are both real strengths and should not blur into a generic "computer repair" message.
